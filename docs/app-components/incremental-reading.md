# Incremental Reading

**Incremental Reading** lets you work through long-form source material a little
at a time, capturing the most important parts as you go and gradually turning them
into study cards. Instead of reading an article in one sitting, you read it across
multiple sessions on a spaced schedule — keeping the most valuable material in
front of you while letting the rest wait.

## How It Works

### 1. Add an Incremental Reading Cell
Create a new cell and choose **Incremental Reading** as the cell type, then import
a source. For now, **websites** are the supported source type — paste the URL of
the page you want to read and Brainy will load its content into the cell.

### 2. Read and Highlight
Work through the imported source and highlight the most important passages as you
read. Each highlight is saved as an **extract** — a snippet of source material set
aside for later processing.

Select the text you want to keep and use the highlight shortcut:

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl + Shift + H` / `Cmd + Shift + H` | Highlight the selected text |

### 3. Finish the Session
When you are done reading for now, you can:

- **Schedule for Later** — set the reading to come back on a future date so you can
  continue where you left off in a later session
- **Mark as Done** — finish the source when you have read everything you need
- **Exit** — leave the session without changing its schedule

## Extracts

Every passage you highlight becomes an **extract**. Extracts are the bridge between
raw source material and finished study cards:

- Go through your extracts one at a time and turn each into a **Cloze** cell
- Use **AI support** to convert an extract into a cloze automatically — Brainy reads
  the extract and produces a ready-to-study cloze deletion for you to keep or refine

See the [Editor](editor.md) for more on cloze cells, and the
[AI Assistant](ai-assistant.md) for more on AI-powered card generation.

## On the Home Screen

The [Home](home.md) dashboard surfaces incremental reading work that needs your
attention through two lists:

- **Incremental Reading Queue** — sources that have passed their due date, ordered by
  **priority** so the most important reading comes first
- **Pending Extracts** — highlighted extracts that still need to be turned into
  cloze cells

This keeps both halves of the workflow — reading and card creation — visible so
nothing you have set aside gets forgotten.
